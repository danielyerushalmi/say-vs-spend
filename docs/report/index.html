Say vs. Spend · evidence report

Did the AI crowd understand shoppers, or just pick the famous brands?

We asked 200 AI shoppers (Flashpoint synthetic personas) about 6 online brands, then checked their “how likely are you to buy” ranking against what the companies really sold (SEC filings). A second panel of 200 different AI shoppers (same persona model) answered the same questions.

I’m open to the idea of buying used clothing if the site feels established and the return policy is clear, but I’m still cautious since I usually stick with brands and retailers I already know.
